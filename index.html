1. 我哋收集啲乜

喺你手機入面（唔會離開）：

  • 你用 Apple「螢幕使用時間」選擇器揀嘅 app 同類別。Apple 只會俾我哋匿名代號（token），我哋見唔到、亦無法得知係邊個 app。

  • 免費次數、永久版購買狀態、環境聲等設定。

以上資料存喺 App 嘅私人容器（App Group），唔會上載、唔會同步到任何伺服器。刪除 App 就會一併刪除。

匿名用量統計（會離開手機）：

為咗知道邊啲功能有人用，Focus Moment 會透過 PostHog 送出少量匿名事件，例如「開咗 App」「開始咗一次遮罩」「睇咗付費頁」。每個事件只包含：事件名稱、模式種類（例如「動態牆」/「鎖定」）、分鐘數、次數、App 版本、iOS 版本、裝置型號、語言、時區、螢幕尺寸、網絡類型（Wi‑Fi／流動數據），以及一個隨機產生嘅匿名安裝識別碼（唔係廣告識別碼；刪除 App 就會重設）。

唔會包含：你揀咗邊啲 app、你寫嘅理由、你嘅名、電郵、電話、聯絡人、位置、廣告識別碼。我哋唔會建立跨 App 嘅個人檔案。我哋已關閉 PostHog 嘅地理位置推斷，並設定 PostHog 唔儲存 IP 地址。

2. 權限

In one line: Focus Moment has no server and no accounts. Which apps you shield, how often, and the reasons you type stay on your phone.

1. What we collect

On your phone (never leaves it):

  • The apps and categories you pick in Apple's Screen Time picker. Apple hands us opaque tokens only — we cannot see or learn which apps they are.
  • Your saved shield sets, session start/end times, and the reasons you write when you unlock for a while.
  • Free-session count, lifetime-unlock status, and settings such as ambient sound.

This lives in the app's private container (App Group). It is never uploaded or synced. Deleting the app deletes it.

Anonymous usage analytics (does leave the phone):

To learn which features are used, Focus Moment sends a small number of anonymous events through PostHog, such as "app opened", "session started", "paywall viewed". Each event carries only: the event name, the kind of mode (e.g. feeds / lockdown), minutes, counts, app version, iOS version, device model, language, time zone, screen size, network type (Wi‑Fi / cellular), and a randomly generated anonymous install identifier (not the advertising identifier; deleting the app resets it).

Never included: which apps you chose, the reasons you typed, your name, email, phone number, contacts, location or advertising identifier. We do not build cross-app profiles. We have disabled PostHog's location inference and configured PostHog not to store IP addresses.

2. Permissions

  • Screen Time (Family Controls): used only to shield the apps you pick. It is not used to monitor or record your usage. You can turn it off at any time in Settings → Screen Time.
  • Notifications: local notifications only — when you tap "Open anyway" on a shield, and when a timed unlock ends. There is no push server.

3. Purchases

The lifetime unlock is handled by the Apple App Store. We never receive your payment details; Apple's privacy policy applies to the transaction.

4. Children

Focus does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13. None of the data above is personally identifiable.

5. Your choices

  • Deleting the app deletes all local data.
  • To have anonymous analytics associated with your device deleted, email us (below) with the app version and approximate dates of use; because the data is anonymous we can only match it on a best-effort basis.

Updates will be posted on this page; material changes will be flagged inside the app.
