支援 · Support

有問題、意見，或者遇到 bug，電郵 toysky731@icloud.com，我哋通常一兩日內回覆。請附上 iOS 版本同 App 版本（設定 → 關於）。

遮罩點解冇生效？

  • 去 iOS「設定 → 螢幕使用時間」確認 Focus Moment 嘅權限仲喺度。iOS 偶然會收回權限；重開 App 會再問你一次。
  • 遮罩需要真正開始一次 session：喺「模式」揀一個模式，撳開始，再揀遮幾耐。
  • 「鎖定」模式係遮住晒所有 app、只留低你嘅名單；如果你要用嘅 app 都被遮，去模式入面加返落名單。

遮住咗但好急要開？

遮罩畫面撳「照開」，會有一個通知跳返入 App，講個理由、揀個時限；時限一到就自動遮返。冇「永久解除」——要停一個模式，返去「模式」撳停止。

免費次數同永久版

  • 頭 10 次手動開始嘅遮罩免費；排定時間自動開始嘅唔計。
  • 永久版係一次過買斷，冇訂閱。買過嘅話，喺付費頁撳「恢復購買」就可以喺新裝置攞返。
  • 退款由 Apple 處理：reportaproblem.apple.com。

刪除資料

Focus Moment 冇戶口、冇伺服器。刪除 App 就會刪除所有本機資料。詳情見私隱政策。

Support (English)

Questions, feedback or bugs: toysky731@icloud.com. We usually reply within a day or two; please include your iOS and app version.

  • Shield not working? Check iOS Settings → Screen Time still lists Focus Moment. iOS occasionally revokes the permission; reopening the app asks again. A shield only applies once you start a session from Modes and pick a duration.
  • Need something urgently? Tap "Open anyway" on the shield. A notification brings you back into the app to give a reason and pick a window; the shield returns when it ends. To stop a mode entirely, go to Modes and stop it.
  • Free sessions and the lifetime unlock. Your first 10 manually started sessions are free. The lifetime unlock is a one-time purchase with no subscription; use "Restore Purchases" on the paywall on a new device. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Deleting data. There are no accounts and no server. Deleting the app deletes everything. See the privacy policy.
